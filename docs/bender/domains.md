# Domains

The target taxonomy of domains includes, but is not limited to:

- `axial1d`
- `torsion_circular1d`
- `beam_planar`
- `truss2d`
- `truss3d`
- `frame2d`
- `frame3d`
- `point_state`
- `stability_column`
- `elasticity_axisymmetric`
- `plate_classical`
- `shell_elementary`

Domains define the mathematical theory, variables, and grammars of the problem, but they do NOT contain the logic to solve them.
